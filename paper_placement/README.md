# Companion article: benchmark-audit paper (paper_placement/)

This directory holds the **standalone companion article** produced by the Phase 4
placement split (D-rulings in `reports/PHASE4_PLACEMENT_SPLIT_PLAN.md`, executed
2026-10-12). It re-frames the graduate-placement component of the project as a
**benchmark audit** and makes **no real-world claims about graduates**.

- `main.tex` — the article (IEEEtran, same class/packages as `paper/main.tex`;
  bibliography is a snapshot copy of `paper/references.bib`).
- `tables/` — tables moved out of `paper/tables/` (git mv, history preserved):
  `tab_descriptive`, `tab_vif`, `tab_logistic`, `tab_model_comparison`,
  `tab_multiseed`, plus the auto-generated placement `tab_experiments`.
- Figures use the shared TikZ macros from `paper/figures/tikz_figs.tex`
  (`\input{../paper/figures/tikz_figs}`); the legacy figure PDFs stay in
  `paper/figures/` and are not referenced here.

## Hypotheses and RQ labels

The companion keeps the original project-wide labels **H4–H6 / RQ4–RQ5**
(cross-referenced as "companion-paper hypotheses") rather than renumbering; the
gap-measurement paper therefore ends with a contiguous RQ1–RQ3 / H1–H3 block and
no gaps (D4). Verdicts here are benchmark properties only:

- H4 (signaling hierarchy): reproduced *on the benchmark* (labels generated).
- H5 (credential locality): **untested** (PLAC01 benchmark of unverified
  provenance, moderate generator signatures).
- H6 (internship returns): not supported *within the benchmark* (STAT01).

## Reporting rulings applied

- Per D3, the **15.34 pp internship claim (INTL01) is not reported** in either
  paper; the artifact stays archived at
  `results/INTL01_internship_effect/metrics.json` (reproduced, unreported).
- Every cohort is disclosed with a provenance tier (moderate: College and
  Global Placement; weak/partial: International Graduates; none found: Student
  Career Success; unverified record in all four cases). Only the India Skills
  file is described as synthetic, on its own source's statement. Evidence:
  `datasets/synthetic_checks_output.json`; inventory:
  `datasets/REAL_VS_SYNTHETIC.csv`).

## Provenance of tables

`tables/tab_experiments.tex` and all other `tab_*.tex` files are generated by
`experiments/make_latex.py` (placement tables are written to the `TABP` output
dir). Do not hand-edit them; regenerate and re-verify
(`experiments/verify.py`, `audit/verify_claims.py`) instead.
