## A. Overlap audit results

Keys used: `job_link` where the schema carries one; normalised `title+company+date+location` composite where it does not; exact full-row hashes for same-schema files. All numbers below are in `datasets/OVERLAP_AUDIT.csv`.

| Dataset | Unique `job_link` | Within-file dups | Nesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| `linkedin_job_postings.csv` | 1,348,454 | 0 | superset of everything below |

| `data-science-job-postings-and-skills/job_postings.csv` | 12,217 | 0 | **100% inside** `linkedin_job_postings`; 39.3% inside `hf_jobs` |

Findings:

1. **The entire HF Skill Gap pair (both tables) is a strict subset of the LinkedIn 2024 Kaggle scrape** — all 519,814 links present in `linkedin_job_postings.csv`. This extends the §A2 finding (which used the skills table only) to the postings table: the HF pair contributes **zero** unique records to the corpus.
2. `data-science-job-postings-and-skills/` (12,218 rows) is a **16th posting dataset on disk that was never in the 15-row manifest**, unused by any experiment, and also a subset of the LinkedIn scrape. Reported for inventory completeness; not added to the headline count.
3. There is exactly **one posting scrape family** in this project (LinkedIn 2024). No genuinely independent market posting corpus is present.

### A.3 USA Job Market Skills

316,279 rows are **aggregate `title × skill keyword × count` triples over 6,328 unique titles**, not individual postings. All 6,328 titles appear as strings among LinkedIn's 578,496 unique `job_title` values (100%), but no company/date columns exist on the USA side, so record identity cannot be established or refuted. The dataset is retained as 316,279 *records* (as before); the "postings" reading of Tier 1 should be understood as aggregate rows on the USA side.

### A.4 Placement / student datasets

Within-file exact-duplicate rows: `intl_graduates` 3; all other seven files 0. Cross-file: no two files share an identifying column (disjoint schemas), so record-level cross-duplication is not evaluable and none is claimed. `fresher_india` and `india_skills_profiles` are candidate/profile-level synthetic files with no posting keys.
